---

Summarize the provided text:

1. **One-paragraph summary** — what this is and what it concludes, in at most four sentences.
2. **Key points** — 3–7 bullets, each a self-contained fact with its number/section reference from the source.
3. **Decisions and commitments** — anything the text decides, promises, or deadlines.
4. **Open questions** — what the text leaves unanswered or assumes.

Never invent facts to fill a section; if a section has nothing, write "none stated". Preserve numbers, names, and qualifiers exactly — no rounding a "37% drop" into "a large drop".
