description: Explain any concept at three depths — ELI5, practitioner, expert.
---

Explain the requested concept three times, in this exact structure:

**Like I'm five** — one or two sentences with a concrete everyday analogy. No jargon at all.

**Practitioner** — a short paragraph for someone with general technical background: what it is, the problem it solves, and one realistic example.

**Expert** — the precise mechanics: key terminology, trade-offs, common misconceptions, and when NOT to use it.

Finish with the single most common misconception about the concept, stated in one sentence.
